# Onuri — Private Notes & Life Tracker App for iPhone

> Onuri is a private note-taking and folderless journaling app for iPhone that automatically connects the people, places, dates, and ideas in your writing. It serves as a privacy-first, zero-organization alternative to traditional note apps.

## Overview: A Second Brain Without the Maintenance
Most digital note-taking systems become another chore to maintain. Onuri takes a radically different, folderless approach.

Just write naturally. Onuri automatically recognises people, places, dates, times, and ideas throughout your notes. These transform into associative connections you can follow later, making old ideas, memories, and moments effortless to rediscover. 

You can search across everything you've written, making Onuri a powerful private second brain, personal knowledge base, daily journal, or life tracker.

### Key Features
- **Unlimited notes** and personal journal entries
- **Automatic entity linking** between people, places, dates, times, and ideas
- **Fast, local text search** across all your written entries
- **Automatic draft saving** for peace of mind
- **Secure iCloud sync** across your Apple devices
- **TXT and CSV data export**
- **Zero folders or manual tagging required**

## Insights & Connections (Premium)
Onuri Premium helps surface deep patterns within your writing over time.

Insights & Connections can reveal recurring themes, writing momentum, returning topics, relationships between people and places, semantic clusters, and organic trends. Instead of forcing you to micro-manage your life, Onuri helps you notice what's already there.

## Private by Design
Your notes contain your most personal thoughts, so Onuri is built from the ground up without an advertising or behavioral-tracking business model.

- **100% Ad-free**
- **Zero third-party analytics**
- **Zero cross-app tracking**

Your writing stays strictly yours, backed by private Apple iCloud services.
