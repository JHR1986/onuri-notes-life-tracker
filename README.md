# Onuri — Private Notes & Life Tracker for iPhone

**Onuri** is a private-first notes and life-tracking app for iPhone that automatically connects the people, places, dates, and ideas in your writing.

Write naturally. Onuri builds useful context around your notes as you go, helping you rediscover related thoughts, memories, people, places, and themes without making organisation another task to maintain.

## A Second Brain Without the Maintenance

Onuri is designed around a simple idea: your notes should become more useful over time without requiring constant organisation.

Just write. Onuri automatically recognises useful context including **people, places, dates, times, and ideas** throughout your notes. These become connections you can follow later, helping related writing surface naturally as your personal archive grows.

That makes Onuri useful as a:

- Private notes app
- Folderless writing space
- Personal knowledge system
- Daily journal
- Life tracker
- Automatic second brain

## Key Features

### Write Freely

Capture ideas, memories, plans, observations, and everyday notes without stopping to decide where everything belongs.

### Automatic Connections

Onuri identifies and connects people, places, dates, times, and ideas found naturally within your writing.

### Fast Search

Search across your notes and return quickly to the context, details, and connections you remember.

### Unlimited Notes

The free tier includes unlimited notes and the core automatic connection experience.

### Automatic Draft Saving

Your writing is saved automatically while you work.

### Private iCloud Sync

Keep your notes available across supported Apple devices through your personal iCloud account.

### TXT & CSV Export

Export your writing whenever you want, helping keep your notes portable and accessible.

## Insights & Connections

**Onuri Premium (€2.99)** adds deeper Insights & Connections designed to surface patterns already present in your writing.

Over time, these insights can help reveal:

- Recurring themes
- Writing momentum
- Returning topics
- Relationships between people and places
- Semantic clusters
- Organic patterns across your notes

The aim is not to create more organisation work. Onuri helps you notice the connections that are already there.

## Private by Design

Personal notes deserve a private space.

Onuri is built without an advertising or behavioural-tracking business model:

- **100% ad-free**
- **Zero third-party analytics**
- **Zero cross-app tracking**
- **App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected**

Your writing stays yours, with private Apple iCloud services available for sync.
