# Onuri — Private Notes That Connect Themselves

**Onuri: Notes & Life Tracker** is a private note-taking app for iPhone that automatically connects the people, places, dates, and ideas in your writing.

Just write. There are no folders to maintain, tags to remember, or systems to organise. As your notes grow, Onuri builds connections between them, making old ideas, memories, people, and moments easier to rediscover.

## A Second Brain Without the Organisation

Most note-taking systems become another thing to maintain. Onuri takes a different approach.

Write naturally and Onuri automatically recognises people, places, dates, times, and ideas throughout your notes. These become connections you can follow later, helping you move between related moments and rediscover things you might otherwise have forgotten.

You can also search across everything you've written, making Onuri useful as a private second brain, personal knowledge base, journal, or simply a place to keep everyday notes.

### Features

- **Unlimited notes and journal entries**
- **Automatic connections** between people, places, dates, times, and ideas
- **Fast text search** across your writing
- **Automatic draft saving**
- **iCloud sync** across your Apple devices
- **TXT and CSV export**
- **No folders or manual tagging required**

## Insights & Connections

Onuri Premium helps surface patterns within your writing.

Insights & Connections can reveal recurring themes, writing momentum, returning topics, relationships between people and places, semantic clusters, and other patterns that emerge naturally over time.

Instead of asking you to track every part of your life, Onuri helps you notice what's already there.

## Private by Design

Your notes can contain some of the most personal information on your phone, so Onuri is deliberately built without an advertising or behavioural-tracking business model.

- **No ads**
- **No analytics**
- **No tracking**

Your writing stays yours.
