## 📱 About Onuri
Onuri is a private notes app that becomes more valuable the more you write. Every note becomes part of a connected collection that's easy to explore, helping you rediscover ideas, memories, and moments over time. 

There's nothing to tag or organise manually. Onuri automatically recognises people, places, dates, times, and ideas throughout your writing, creating connections that make your notes effortless to revisit. Find anything you've written using fast text search or by following the related connections between the people, places, and topics that appear throughout your notes.

### Free Features
* **Unlimited notes and journal entries**
* **Automatic connections as you write**
* **Fast text search** with intelligent connection filtering
* **Automatic draft saving**
* **iCloud sync** across your Apple devices
* **Export your writing** as TXT or CSV

### Onuri Premium
Discover patterns hidden within your writing. Premium unlocks **Insights and Connections**, revealing recurring themes, writing momentum, returning topics, meaningful relationships between people and places, semantic clusters, and more—all generated automatically from your own notes. Instead of asking you to track your life, Onuri helps you notice it.

### Built for Privacy
Private by design:
* **No ads**
* **No analytics**
* **No tracking**

Write freely, knowing your thoughts stay yours.
