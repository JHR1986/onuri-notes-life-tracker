## 📱 About Onuri: Private Notes, Life Tracker & Automatic Connections
**Onuri: Private Notes, Life Tracker & Automatic Connections** is a secure private notes app built to become more valuable the more you write. Every note becomes part of an interconnected digital collection that's easy to explore, helping you rediscover ideas, memories, and moments over time. 

There's nothing to tag or organise manually. Onuri automatically recognises people, places, dates, times, and ideas throughout your writing, creating automatic connections that make your notes effortless to revisit. Find anything you've written using fast text search or by following the related connections between the people, places, and topics that appear throughout your notes.

### Free Features of Onuri: Notes & Life Tracker
* **Unlimited notes and journal entries** inside your **Onuri iOS app**
* **Automatic connections as you write** for seamless second-brain organization
* **Fast text search** with intelligent connection filtering
* **Automatic draft saving**
* **iCloud sync** across your Apple devices
* **Export your writing** as TXT or CSV

### Onuri Premium
Discover patterns hidden within your writing. Premium unlocks **Insights and Connections**, revealing recurring themes, writing momentum, returning topics, meaningful relationships between people and places, semantic clusters, and more—all generated automatically from your own notes. Instead of asking you to track your life, Onuri helps you notice it.

### Built for Privacy
Private by design:
* **No ads**
* **No analytics**
* **No tracking**

Write freely, knowing your thoughts stay yours.
