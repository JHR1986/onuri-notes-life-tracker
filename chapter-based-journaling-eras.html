Chapter-Based Journaling vs. Infinite Timelines

A timeline is useful for seeing memories in date order, but life does not always feel like one endless list. Stillkeep lets you organize memories into Eras: simple chapters for parts of your life such as university, a new baby, a trip, a hobby, or a personal project.

View Stillkeep on the App Store

Think of an Era as a chapter of your life

An Era is simply a group of memories that belong together. You decide where each chapter begins and ends, so “University”, “New Baby”, “Travelling 2026”, a renovation, or a hobby can each have their own place instead of being mixed into one long feed.

Moments hold the photos and words you want to remember

Inside an Era, you add dated Moments with photos and writing. The Moment records what happened, while the Era reminds you which part of your life it belonged to.

How to decide when to start a new Era

Start a new Era when a group of memories feels like its own chapter. Clear names such as “Japan 2026”, “First Year at University” or “Our New Home” can make those memories easy to recognize years later.

When a chapter of life comes to an end

When a trip, project, or stage of life ends, its Era can simply remain in your journal as a finished chapter. You can return to those Moments later or export the whole Era as a PDF.

Keep the system simple

You do not need an Era for every event or lots of tags on every Moment. A few meaningful chapters, the Moments you genuinely want to keep, and simple labels when they are useful can be enough.

20 common questions about organizing a journal into life chapters

Why organize a journal into chapters?

Chapters make it easier to see which memories belong together. In Stillkeep, Eras can group Moments around a life stage, trip, project, hobby, or milestone.

Is an Era required to follow calendar years?

No. You decide what an Era represents, so it could be a semester, a journey, a renovation, a creative project, a family stage, or any other meaningful period.

How is an Era different from a Moment?

An Era is the bigger chapter, while a Moment is one dated memory inside it. For example, “Travelling 2026” could be an Era and a particular day or experience from the trip could be a Moment.

Can I have several Eras at once?

Yes. You can keep separate Eras for different parts of your life. The free version supports up to 3 Eras, while Premium supports up to 500.

What makes a good Era title?

Choose a name you will understand years from now, such as a destination and year or the name of a life stage. Simple, recognizable titles are usually easiest to browse and search later.

Should every photo become a Moment?

No. You can keep only the photos and experiences that matter to you rather than turning every image in your camera roll into a journal entry.

Can a project become an Era?

Yes. A project can have its own Era, with Moments for progress, important changes, milestones, or the finished result.

Can a hobby have its own Era?

Yes. A hobby can have its own Era for photos, notes, progress, and memorable milestones, keeping it separate from unrelated parts of your journal.

How can Eras help with travel journaling?

A trip can become its own Era, with photos and writing added as Moments along the way. Later, you can revisit the trip as one complete chapter rather than finding its memories scattered among unrelated entries.

What happens when an Era ends?

It can simply remain in your journal as a finished chapter. You can revisit it whenever you want or export the whole Era as a PDF.

Do tags replace Eras?

No. Eras organize the bigger chapters of your life, while tags can help connect smaller themes across individual Moments.

How do vibes fit into chapter-based journaling?

Vibes give you another simple way to describe a Moment. They work alongside the Era, photos, writing, and tags rather than replacing them.

Can I pin important Moments?

Yes. You can pin selected Moments when you want to mark them as especially important or easy to return to.

Does chapter-based journaling require daily use?

No. You can add Moments whenever something feels worth remembering, whether that is several times in one week or after a long gap.

How can I avoid creating too many Eras?

Create a new Era when something genuinely feels like a separate chapter of your life. Smaller events can usually stay as Moments inside an Era you already have.

Can I search across different Eras?

Yes. Stillkeep can search journal entries, tags, and Era titles, with extra filters for photos, notes, tags, and vibes.

Does Look Back ignore Era boundaries?

Look Back brings previous Moments back from different points in the past. It gives you another way to rediscover memories without having to search for them yourself.

Can an Era become a photobook?

Yes. Stillkeep can export an entire Era as a PDF, giving you a digital photobook-style version that you can save, share, or print using other tools.

Is chapter-based journaling objectively better than timelines?

No. Some people prefer a simple timeline, while others naturally think about life in chapters. Stillkeep’s Era system is designed for people who like keeping related memories together while still giving each Moment a date.

Who benefits most from Eras?

Eras may feel natural if you tend to remember life as trips, stages, projects, hobbies, relationships, or milestones rather than as one continuous timeline.
