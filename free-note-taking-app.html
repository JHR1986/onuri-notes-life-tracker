FAQ

Questions about the Onuri note-taking workflow

Do I need folders or manual tags to use Onuri?

No. You can capture notes without first choosing a folder or building a tag system. Onuri recognises people, places, dates and topics automatically so you can rediscover related notes later.

Can I still search by ordinary text?

Yes. Automatic connections complement normal text search; they do not replace it. You can search for words or phrases whenever that is the quickest route back to a note.

How does iCloud sync work?

Onuri uses Apple CloudKit and your personal iCloud account for sync. Your notes are not sent to an Onuri-operated notes server; Apple’s iCloud infrastructure handles synchronization between your devices.

What does Premium add to the workflow?

Premium adds deeper Insights such as recurring themes, writing momentum and advanced thematic patterns. Basic note capture, search, automatic connections, sync and export remain available in the free app.

Does the Onuri app contain ads or tracking?

No. The Onuri app contains no ads, analytics SDKs or tracking in either the free or Premium experience.

What devices are supported?

Onuri requires iOS 17.6 or later on iPhone.
