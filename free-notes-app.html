FAQ

Free notes app questions

Is Onuri really free to use?

Yes. Onuri's core features—including unlimited notes, automatic connection recognition, fast text search, draft saving, iCloud sync, and TXT or CSV export—are 100% free with no time limits.

Does the free version have a note limit?

No. You can write as many notes and journal entries as you like without hitting any paywalls or caps.

How does Onuri sync across my devices without a server?

Onuri uses Apple's native CloudKit and your personal iCloud account. Your data goes directly between your devices through Apple's infrastructure, ensuring absolute privacy.

What does Onuri Premium unlock?

Onuri Premium unlocks advanced Insights and deeper connection analytics that surface recurring themes, writing momentum, returning topics, and meaningful relationships across your notes.

Are there any ads or trackers in the free version?

None at all. Onuri has zero ads, zero analytics SDKs, and zero tracking in both the free and premium versions.

What devices are supported?

Onuri requires iOS 17.6 or later on iPhone.
