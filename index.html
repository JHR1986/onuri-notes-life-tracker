FAQ

Questions about Onuri

What is Onuri: Notes & Life Tracker?

Onuri is a private notes app for iPhone that automatically connects the people, places, dates and ideas in your writing as you type. There are no folders or manual tags to maintain.

What is a second brain app?

A second brain is a digital system for storing and retrieving ideas, notes and memories. Onuri approaches this by creating useful connections automatically as you write.

Is Onuri free to use?

Yes. Onuri's free features include unlimited notes and journal entries, automatic connections, fast text search, automatic draft saving, iCloud sync and TXT or CSV export. Onuri Premium adds Insights and Connections.

Does Onuri collect my data?

No. Onuri's App Store privacy information states that data is not collected. Onuri has no ads, analytics or tracking, and uses your personal iCloud account for sync across Apple devices.

Is Onuri an AI second brain?

Onuri uses private on-device intelligence to recognise and connect information in your writing. Its core experience does not depend on uploading your notes to a cloud AI service.

How is Onuri different from folder-based note taking?

Instead of asking you to decide where every note belongs, Onuri recognises people, places, dates, times and ideas throughout your writing so you can search and follow connections later.

What devices does Onuri support?

Onuri requires iOS 17.6 or later and uses iCloud to sync across your Apple devices.
