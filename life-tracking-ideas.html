Life tracking reference

What Can You Track in a Life Tracker?

Life is not one metric. It is people you meet, places you return to, conversations you want to remember, decisions you make, projects you finish, ideas you nearly forget and ordinary moments that become meaningful later.

This reference contains 100 practical examples of things a person might choose to remember in natural notes. It is not a checklist, and Onuri does not automatically monitor your life. You decide what to write; Onuri helps connect recognised people, places, dates and ideas so related context can be rediscovered later.

How to use this page: browse for ideas, not obligations. A useful life log can be one sentence long. These examples use ordinary language rather than forms, tags or rigid categories.
