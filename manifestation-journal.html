Intention & Goal Journal FAQ

What is a visual intention and manifestation journal?

A visual manifestation journal is a structured personal diary that combines imagery (such as goal photos, milestone pictures, or vision board assets) with written reflections and action steps to keep you focused.

How does Stillkeep help with goal tracking?

Stillkeep provides a calm, distraction-free environment where you group photos and written reflections into visual Eras and Moments, replacing messy camera rolls with clear, intentional life chapters.

How is my goal and journal data handled?

Stillkeep’s published Privacy Policy says the app does not use third-party analytics, third-party advertising SDKs, or cross-app tracking. User data is stored and processed using Apple infrastructure, including CloudKit for storage and synchronization.
