Digital notebook questions

Can I keep separate notebooks for work and personal life?

Onuri uses one writing stream instead of separate notebooks. The people, places and topics in each note act as the dividers, so work and personal notes stay easy to separate when you filter.

Will my notebook slow down as it grows?

Onuri is designed for archives of hundreds of entries. Search, filters and related entries are how you move through a large notebook without scrolling page by page.
