A calmer iPhone notepad

A notepad that remembers the context.

Onuri is a private notepad for iPhone built for quick writing without the filing. Capture text, notes, thoughts and ideas as they happen; Onuri connects useful context automatically so your writing is easier to find later.

Write first

Use Onuri as a simple writing pad for quick notes, drafts, thoughts and everyday text. There are no folders to choose before you start.

More than a text editor

People, places, dates and topics in your notes become useful connections. Your notepad gradually becomes a searchable record of what you were thinking about.

Private on iPhone

Onuri is an iPhone app with no ads, tracking or third-party analytics. Notes can sync privately through your personal iCloud account.

A simple notepad when you need to write. A connected second brain when you want to find it again.

Onuri keeps the experience simple: capture what matters, let useful context connect itself, and come back through search when you need it.
