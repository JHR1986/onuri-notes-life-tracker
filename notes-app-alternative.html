Why choose Onuri as your notes app alternative?

Your notes become more useful when the context around them stays visible. Onuri is designed to preserve that context while keeping everyday writing simple.

Automatic connections

Onuri identifies people, places, dates and ideas in your writing, helping related notes come together naturally over time.

Folderless by design

Write first. Onuri's connections and search give you another way to navigate your notes without making organization the main task.

Fast full-text search

Search across your notes whenever you remember a phrase, person, place or detail and want to return to the original context.

Private iCloud sync

Keep your notes available across your Apple devices through your personal iCloud account while maintaining a privacy-focused experience.

Unlimited notes

Build a useful personal archive without treating every new thought as something that needs to fit into a fixed structure.

Portable writing

Export your notes when you want them outside the app, helping keep your writing accessible and under your control.

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Onuri a useful notes app alternative?

Onuri is designed around natural writing and automatic connections. It identifies people, places, dates and ideas inside your notes so related memories and thoughts are easier to rediscover.

Can I use Onuri without organizing notes into folders?

Yes. Onuri supports a folderless workflow, allowing you to write first and use automatic connections and search to find related notes later.

Can I export my notes from Onuri?

Yes. Onuri supports exporting your notes so your writing remains portable and accessible.
