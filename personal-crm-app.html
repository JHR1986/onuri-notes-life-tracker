Why a Personal CRM Can Be Simpler Than a CRM

A personal CRM is a way to preserve useful context about the people in your life. That might mean remembering what a friend is working on, the last conversation you had with a client, where you met someone, a recommendation they gave you, or a detail you want to revisit later.

Onuri approaches that problem through natural notes rather than structured contact records. You do not need to create a profile, choose fields, maintain a pipeline, or decide where every detail belongs. You simply write the context while it is fresh, and Onuri recognises useful entities in the text.

This makes Onuri especially suited to people who want the memory benefits of a personal CRM without turning friendships, professional relationships or everyday conversations into administrative work.

Remember Conversations, Friends, Clients and Shared Context

Relationship memory is rarely one neat record. A person can appear across months or years of notes: a coffee, a work meeting, a trip, an idea they shared, a birthday plan, a project decision, or a place you visited together. Onuri lets those fragments remain individual notes while still becoming connected through recurring people, places, dates and topics.

  • Friends and family: keep small details, plans, recommendations and moments you want to remember.
  • Clients and collaborators: preserve meeting context, decisions, project details and follow-up ideas in your own private notes.
  • Professional relationships: remember where you met, what you discussed and the topics you have in common.
  • Everyday conversations: capture something important without building a formal contact record around it.

When a name or related topic appears again, connected notes can bring older context back into view. The result is less about managing contacts and more about remembering the history around people.

A Relationship Notes App That Connects Context Automatically

Traditional relationship tracking often depends on manual tags, custom fields, reminders or carefully maintained records. Onuri uses on-device recognition to identify useful entities as you write. People, places, dates and recurring topics can become pathways back to related notes without requiring you to build those links by hand.

For example, a note mentioning a person and a project can later connect with another note mentioning the same person, a related place, or the project itself. You can follow those connections to rediscover why a name matters rather than relying on memory for the exact wording of an old note.

This same automatic linking is the foundation of Onuri’s automatic second-brain workflow and its folderless approach to note-taking.

Private Relationship Notes Stay Personal

Notes about other people can contain sensitive personal context, which makes privacy particularly important. Onuri processes note content on device and does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs inside the app. Notes can sync through your personal iCloud account rather than an Onuri-operated note-content server.

You can also export your writing as standard TXT or CSV files, helping you keep control of the information you have captured. For the full privacy-focused workflow, see Onuri as a private notes app.

Personal CRM Notes You Can Capture Naturally

After a conversation

Write two sentences about what mattered: a new job, a recommendation, a project update, a place someone wants to visit, or a question you want to remember next time.

After a client or project meeting

Capture the decision and the people involved in ordinary prose. Later, the person, project or date can help lead you back to that note.

When someone recommends something

Write the person and recommendation together. The relationship between who suggested it and what they suggested remains part of the note instead of being split across apps.

When context accumulates over time

Let separate notes stay separate. Onuri’s connections help the history around a person emerge from the writing itself rather than from one continuously maintained profile.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a personal CRM app?

A personal CRM app helps you remember useful context about people and relationships. Onuri takes a note-first approach: you write naturally about conversations, friends, clients or collaborators, and related people, places, dates and topics can connect across your notes.

Can Onuri be used for notes about clients and professional contacts?

Yes. You can use Onuri for your own private notes about meetings, decisions, projects and professional relationships. It is a personal note-taking tool rather than a sales pipeline or shared team CRM.

Do I have to create contact profiles or fill in fields?

No. Onuri is designed around natural writing. You do not need to maintain custom contact fields, folders or manual wiki links in order to capture relationship context.

How does Onuri help me remember past conversations?

Names, places, dates and topics recognised in your notes create useful connections. Those connections and full-text search can help you rediscover previous notes even when you do not remember the exact wording.

Are relationship notes private?

Onuri processes note content on device, contains no advertising trackers or third-party analytics SDKs in the app, supports private iCloud sync, and lets you export notes as TXT or CSV files.
