Photo journal reference

What Should You Put in a Photo Journal?

A photo becomes more valuable when the story around it survives too: who was there, where it happened, why the moment mattered and the small details a camera cannot explain.

This reference contains 100 practical photo-journal ideas. It is inspiration, not a checklist. Keep what matters to you: a photograph, a few words of context, and enough detail for your future self or family to understand the memory.

How to use this page: browse for ideas and adapt them to your own life. You do not need to document everything. The strongest memory archives often preserve ordinary moments alongside major milestones.
