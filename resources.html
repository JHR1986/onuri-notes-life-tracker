Which guide answers your question?

Each guide covers one question in depth. Pick the one closest to what you are trying to do.

  • “I just want somewhere fast to jot things down.” → Notepad for quick capture, or Notes App for the full everyday overview.
  • “My folders have become a mess.” → Folderless Notes explains how to retrieve notes without a filing system.
  • “I already use Apple Notes.” → Notes App Alternative compares the two approaches honestly.
  • “What do I get without paying?” → Free Notes App lists exactly what is free and what Premium adds.
  • “I want to remember people and conversations.” → Personal CRM.
  • “I want a record of my life, not just notes.” → Life Tracker, then 100 life tracking ideas.
  • “Where does my data go?” → Private Notes App and the technical capabilities page.
  • “I don’t know which note-taking method suits me.” → Note-taking methods + quiz.
