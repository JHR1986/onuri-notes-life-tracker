Questions

What are the most important things to remember?

For most people: the people they care about and what's happening in their lives, important dates like birthdays and renewals, routines that change by day, and passing comments such as gift ideas and recommendations.

What's the easiest way to remember things?

Write them down the moment they happen, in a sentence, with who, where and when. The surrounding context is what helps you find the note again later.

How can 20 things combine in over a million ways?

Each of the 20 things can either be included or left out. That's 2 to the power of 20 combinations, or 1,048,576. Take away the empty set and the 20 single items, and 1,048,555 combinations bring together two or more things.

Where should I write these things down?

Anywhere you will actually look again: a paper notebook, the notes app on your phone, or a private notes app such as Onuri. What matters most is writing each thing as a short sentence with who, where and when.
