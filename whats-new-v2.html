The v2 features in detail

What each change means in daily use.

A closer look at the main additions in version 2 and where you will find them in the app.

Home-screen filters

Recognised people, places, dates, times and topics now sit on the home screen. Tap one to narrow your history to the notes that mention it, then add a second filter to narrow further.

Connected terms

When you filter, Onuri shows terms that often appear in the same notes. They are a quick way to follow a thread you did not know was there.

Related entries

Open any note to see others with a similar meaning. This helps when an older note used different words for the same idea.

Light and dark mode

The whole app now follows a light or dark appearance, free for everyone.

Ongoing and Done (Premium)

Mark notes that track something in progress and filter by status, useful for plans, projects and follow-ups written as ordinary notes.

Themes and icons (Premium)

Meadow, Dusk, Ocean, Ink and Blossom each come in light and dark variants, with a matching alternate app icon.

Questions about v2

Is v2 a free update?

Yes. The v2 home-screen filters, connected terms, related entries and dark mode are part of the free app. Premium features are optional.

Which devices and languages does v2 support?

Onuri v2 runs on iPhone with iOS 17.6 or later and is available in English, Japanese and Korean.

Write naturally. Find everything again.

Onuri remains private by design, with on-device processing, notes stored on-device and synced through your iCloud, and no ads, analytics or tracking in the app.
